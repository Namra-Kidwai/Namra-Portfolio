A little about how I work

I don't just fill reqs — I build the teams that keep the systems running, patch the CVE, and close the deal. Four years deep in India IT recruiting, across cybersecurity, IAM, cloud, DevOps, and — right now — ServiceNow.

I'm currently running client acquisition and recruitment operations at Fourways Consulting — bringing new enterprise clients on board and building the hiring engine that delivers for them, technical and non-IT hiring alike. I've worked both sides of the table: in-house corporate recruitment and agency recruitment for external clients, so I know how hiring looks from either seat.

In two years at Cambay Healthcare I kept new-hire retention above 85%. Across four years I've recruited into more than ten different technical domains, all within India. My thing is precision over volume: the right candidate, not just an available one.
